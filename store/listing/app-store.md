## Description (max 4000)

```
Kluvs is a home for the books you love and the people you love talking about them with. Gather a club, choose what to read next, and keep the conversation going long after the last page.

BRING YOUR PEOPLE
Start a club in a moment and share an invite link or code. Everyone you bring lands in the same place: who's in, what you're reading, and when you'll meet.

READ A BOOK TOGETHER
Each session has a book, a due date, and a discussion timeline. Join the read or sit one out, and see at a glance how many of your club are reading along.

KEEP THE CONVERSATION GOING
Plan discussions ahead of time, RSVP when you'll be there, and jot down notes for upcoming sessions, so the best thoughts from the margins make it to the meeting.

BRING YOUR BOOKS
Search for any book by title or author, then place it on your shelf: Want to Read, Currently Reading, Read, or Not Finished. Like the ones that stayed with you.

KEEP A READING LIFE
Your profile holds your reading log, what's on your shelf right now, and the story of your reading so far: books finished, clubs joined, and how long you've been reading with Kluvs.

NO CLUB? NO PROBLEM
Kluvs works just as well for a reader on their own. Shelve, log, and track your reading, then bring a club in whenever you're ready.

Kluvs also works on the web, and clubs that live on Discord can add the companion bot.

Bring your books. Bring your people. Read together.

Reading is done best together.
```
